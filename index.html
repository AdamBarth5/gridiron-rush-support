Common questions

I bought something but didn't get it

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases in the Bundles section. This restores the Starter Pack and Remove Ads on the same Apple ID. Gem packs are delivered right after purchase; if one didn't arrive, contact us with the date of the purchase.

What does Remove Ads do?

It turns off the ads that play between games, for good. Reward ads (free gems, coin doubling, free gear draws) stay optional, so you can still watch them for bonuses whenever you like.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your save, so keep the app installed if you want to keep your progress. Purchases can always be restored with Restore Purchases.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EU, UK and other regions that need consent, open Settings in the game and tap Privacy options.

How do the gear draws work?

Gear draws give a random grade. The odds for every draw are shown in the game: open the Gear board and tap Odds next to the draw buttons.
