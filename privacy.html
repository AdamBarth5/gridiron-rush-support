Privacy Policy

Gridiron Rush: Idle Football · Effective November 16, 2026

The short version

  • No account and no name or email collected by the game. Signing in to Game Center for the leaderboards is optional and handled by Apple.
  • Your save file stays on your iPhone. From version 1.0.2 the App sends anonymous gameplay statistics (for example, which game you reached and whether you won) so we can balance the game. Never your name.
  • Ads are non-personalized, and the game never asks to track you (no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no advertising identifier).
  • We use three service providers: Google AdMob for ads, Google Analytics for Firebase for gameplay statistics and RevenueCat for purchases and reward verification. They receive limited technical data, described below.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Who we are

This policy covers the iPhone app Gridiron Rush: Idle Football (the "App") and this website, provided by Barthventures ("we", "us"). We're responsible for the personal information processed through the App as described here. Questions? See Contact.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress and settings locally on your iPhone: games and stars, coins, gems, training, cosmetics, gear, trophies, missions, your in-game player name, and settings such as sound and frame rate. This data isn't sent to us. It's included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer) under your Apple account, and it's deleted when you delete the App.

Notifications: if you allow them, the App schedules local reminders on your iPhone (for example "your camp is full"). No push-notification token is sent to us.

Information collected by service providers

Google AdMob (advertising)

The App shows ads between games and optional reward ads through Google AdMob. The App requests non-personalized ads and doesn't request access to your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process:

  • your IP address, which can indicate an approximate (city-level) location;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language, app version and an app-specific device identifier that is not the advertising identifier;
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown, tapped or completed;
  • diagnostic information, such as SDK errors and performance.

Where required by law (including the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App shows Google's consent message before ads load, and you can change your choice any time in the App: Settings → Privacy options. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics for Firebase (gameplay statistics)

From version 1.0.2 the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, so we can fix difficulty spikes, improve features and see which parts players enjoy. It may process:

  • a random app-instance ID created by the Firebase SDK (reset when you delete the App; not your Apple ID, name or email, and not the advertising identifier, which the App never uses);
  • gameplay events: games started and finished (game number, score, result, time played), training, Locker, Shop and other menu actions, reward ads watched and in-app purchases made (product and price, no payment details);
  • basic device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language) and an approximate location (country or region) that Google derives from the connection's IP address.

Analytics data is not used for advertising or to track you across apps. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, the consent message shown at launch also covers analytics: if you decline storing data on your device, the App sends only anonymous, ID-free signals. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy options. Learn more: Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat (purchases and reward verification)

RevenueCat processes in-app purchases and verifies reward ads so items can be delivered and restored. It may process:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the App (not linked to your name, email or Apple ID);
  • purchase history and transaction details provided by Apple (product, price, currency, date, country);
  • basic device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language);
  • reward-ad events (for example, that a reward ad was completed) and the gems granted for it.

Learn more: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Apple

Payments, refunds and App Store downloads are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment card details or your Apple ID. If you've enabled sharing with developers, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated app analytics and crash reports.

Apple Game Center (leaderboards)

From version 1.0.2 the App has optional leaderboards. If you're signed in to Game Center, the App sends your weekly score and your best Endless Gauntlet depth to Game Center, and shows other players' Game Center nicknames and scores. Your Game Center nickname and scores can be seen by other players on those leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy: we don't receive or store your Game Center details, and the App doesn't pass them to our other service providers. You can sign out or change your Game Center settings in Settings › Game Center on your iPhone, and parents can limit or turn off Game Center with Screen Time. If you don't sign in, the leaderboards still work on your iPhone without sending anything.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data (such as IP address) when you visit; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. Bug reports you choose to post as GitHub Issues are public and handled under GitHub's terms, so please don't include personal information in them; use email for anything private. When you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and use it only to reply and help you.

How information is used

  • To show ads, limit their frequency and grant the rewards for ads you choose to watch.
  • To process, deliver, restore and verify in-app purchases, including Remove Ads.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems.
  • To answer your support and privacy requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We don't use your information for personalized advertising, we don't build profiles about you, and we don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Legal bases (EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Contract: processing purchases and delivering what you bought.
  • Consent: storing or accessing information on your device for advertising, where consent is required. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the App secure, preventing fraud, measuring and fixing ad delivery, and answering support requests.
  • Legal obligation: keeping transaction records required by law.

Sharing & selling

We share information only with the service providers above, to run the App; with authorities when required by law; or as part of a merger or sale of the App, in which case this policy continues to apply. We don't sell personal information, and we don't "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law (CCPA/CPRA), because ads are non-personalized.

How long we keep it

  • On-device game data: until you delete the App.
  • Purchase and reward records (RevenueCat): for as long as needed to restore your purchases and meet legal and accounting requirements.
  • Advertising data (Google): according to Google's retention policies.
  • Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): event-level data for up to 14 months; after that only aggregated reports remain.
  • Support requests: as long as needed to resolve them. Public GitHub issues stay until deleted.

Your choices & rights

  • Ad consent: change or withdraw it any time in the App (Settings → Privacy options), where available.
  • Notifications: turn them off in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Gridiron Rush.
  • Delete your game data: delete the App. There's no server copy of your progress.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection or restriction: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may have these rights over data held about you. Because the App doesn't collect your name or email, we may need the anonymous app user ID to find your records. Contact us and we'll help.
  • No discrimination: we won't treat you differently for exercising your rights.
  • Complaints: you can also contact your local data-protection authority.

Children

The App is rated 9+ on the App Store but is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Ads in the App are non-personalized. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data sent by the App to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Game progress stays on your device and is protected by your iPhone's own security. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, they use appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App adds features. We'll post the new version here with a new effective date, and for significant changes we'll give notice in the App or on this page.
