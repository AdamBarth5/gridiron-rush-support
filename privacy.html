Summary

Gridiron Rush: Idle Football ("the app") is made by Barthventures ("we"). There is no account and no sign-in. We don't sell your personal information and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app uses two service providers: Google AdMob to show ads and RevenueCat to handle in-app purchases and verify rewarded ads.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, gems, cosmetics, settings and statistics) is saved only on your iPhone. We don't receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Information collected by service providers

Google AdMob (ads)

The app requests non-personalized ads and never asks for permission to track you, so it does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may still process information such as your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and operating-system details, and how you interact with ads. In the EU, UK and other regions where it's required, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads load. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings under Privacy options. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

RevenueCat (purchases and rewarded ads)

When you make an in-app purchase or watch a rewarded ad, RevenueCat processes a random anonymous app user ID created by the app, your purchase history and receipts from Apple, basic device information, and rewarded-ad events, so that purchases can be delivered and restored and rewarded ads can be verified. It is not linked to your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Apple

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

How information is used

  • To show ads and give you rewards for the ads you choose to watch.
  • To deliver, restore and verify in-app purchases, including Remove Ads.
  • To keep the app working and fix problems.

Children

The app is rated 9+ and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy options (where available).
  • Delete the app to remove all game data stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete data held by our service providers. Contact us and we'll help you make a request.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions or requests: contact us.
